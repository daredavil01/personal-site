      Sources.

      Two counting methods. Substack publishes its own wordcount per post and that number is taken as-is. WordPress.com exposes no equivalent field, so those counts are derived by stripping HTML from the post body and splitting on whitespace. The two are close but not identical measurements, and each post records which produced its figure.
