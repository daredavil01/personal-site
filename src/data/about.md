# 5 minutes

## The engineer

I write software for a living: full-stack web apps first, data pipelines now. At Bridgenext I run the ETL behind DORA metrics, from GitLab and event APIs through SQL into dashboards. After hours I ship small, sourced, accessible websites, mostly for causes I care about, and this site is the one I keep coming back to.

## The runner

Running started as a way to stay sane and became the spine of my week, five days of training at it. I have raced everything from 10K to a 50K ultra, and every race, bib and finishing time is logged here.

## The trekker

The forts of the Sahyadri are where I go to be small. Some are a two-hour walk, some took a whole night: the Panhala–Pawankhind route took twenty-two hours. Each fort here has its own photos and notes.

## The reader and writer

I read in English and Marathi — technology, public policy, philosophy, fiction, sport — and I write to find out what I think: essays on Substack and WordPress, the 100 Days to Offload challenge, more than a thousand short posts, and Dare Write's, a podcast on books, technology and travel.

## The citizen

I care about how technology meets society. I studied technology policy at the Takshashila Institution and was a fellow of its first NAST cohort, researching citizen agency when AI systems are deployed in public. I volunteer with social organisations like Nirman, and build tools for foundations working in rural Maharashtra.

# 10 minutes

Since college I have been pulled in many directions at once: technology, society, psychology, blogging, design, video editing, digital well-being. It took a while to see that as a strength rather than indecision. All that exploration taught me what I am good at, what I am not, what I like, what I do not, and what I want my work to be.

What I want is simple to say and hard to do: engage critically with what is changing in technology, become a little better every day, understand what is happening around me, and enjoy a meaningful, purposeful journey while doing it.

Today that means a full-time job as a software engineer at Bridgenext, focused on full-stack development and data engineering, alongside training for marathons and ultras. Before Bridgenext I spent three years as a software engineer at Emtec Inc., building an internal developer portal. Before that, at RIT in Sangli, I led the Media Cell's design team, designed for TEDxRajarambapuIT and ran social media for VivekVahini.

In 2024 I studied technology policy at the Takshashila Institution and joined the first cohort of its Network for Advanced Study of Technology Geopolitics, researching how citizens keep agency when AI is deployed in public life. That question still shapes what I build: data stories where every number links to its source, tools that work offline and in Marathi, sites that score 100 on accessibility.

If you are interested in things across domains, we will probably get along. Let us talk, nourish our interests, and learn from each other.
